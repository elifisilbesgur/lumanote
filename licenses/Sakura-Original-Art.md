# Sakura collection — artwork provenance

All 30 pixel models in `tools/ui/sakura-art.js`, their thumbnail poses and the animation code in `sakura-garden.js` were authored programmatically for this LumaNote update. The user's attached pictures were treated as visual/subject references. Their pixels, watermark-bearing images, logos, font files and third-party audio were not embedded in this collection.

Subject list: gate, bonsai trees, koi and frog ponds, bridge, bamboo objects, rock garden, stepping stones, lanterns, festive garland, tea pavilion, picnic, fish streamers, chime, iris flowers and six indoor furnishings. Festival lantern objects are grouped as decorative lanterns rather than presented as a historical or ethnographic reconstruction.

No external art-library licence is needed for files copied from a provider because no such files were copied into this collection. This document records provenance; it is not a claim of copyright eligibility, exclusive rights or a legal opinion.

Existing audio and previous asset licence files are unchanged; this release adds no downloaded audio.
